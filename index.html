How pressure and the rating work

Pressure Index. A calibrated win-probability model scores every ball. Pressure Index is the expected absolute change in win probability on the next ball, scaled so 1.0 is an average ball. Pressure balls are the top 20%.

Level is win probability added per 100 pressure balls. Consistency is the share of pressure innings or spells with positive impact. Resilience compares pressure impact with what the player's own ordinary-situation form predicts. All three are shrunk toward the league average to protect against small samples.

Top-10 chance is the share of 300 match-level bootstraps in which the player stayed in the top 10 (available for the leading 25).
